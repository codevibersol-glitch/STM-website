## See, decide, act, learn: how STM plays

_How STM works_ - Many times a second, STM reads the game, picks the cheapest goal it can run, acts on it, and keeps notes on what happened. Each part below has a small working model you can click: the same ideas as STM, with sample data.

The loop:

- **See** - STM's addon shows the game's state as small coloured cells in a corner of the screen: health, the target, the bags, the fight. STM reads them from the screen, many times a second.
- **Decide** - Every goal has a cost. Of the goals that can run right now, the cheapest one wins, and every other goal says why it waited.
- **Act** - Key presses and clicks, like a player, and paths over a navmesh: a map of the ground with every mob camp priced in.
- **Learn** - Every fight goes into a ledger, every decision into a feed, and every death into a post-mortem. The fixes come from them.

### Goals: The cheapest goal that can run wins

STM keeps a ladder of goals, each with a cost: staying alive costs least, walking costs most. Every moment, each goal checks whether it can run, and the cheapest one that can, does. Change the world below and watch the choice move, with the reason every other goal waited.

- Survival first, then the fight, rest, errands, the next pull, and last the walk
- A tie goes to the order in your profile: repair before selling
- A flee runs only toward a guard it can reach alive

### Score: Where to level: the area worth the most an hour

For every level, the grind plan scores the areas STM can walk to by the experience an hour they are worth: the kills an hour it can make there, what each kill gives at your level, and the time lost walking. Mobs above your level cap are not targets, and neither are mobs standing in deep water. Pick a level and watch the winner move.

- One number decides: experience an hour
- Never above the level cap your profile sets
- Learned the hard way: a bay of crocolisks scored high, was fought swimming, and cost a character

### Maps: Camps are priced like walls, roads like a shortcut

STM walks on a navmesh, a map of the ground it can stand on. Open ground costs three times a road, and the ground around a mob camp costs so much that the path bends round it: a longer walk beats a fight with four mobs at once. The same pricing guides sell trips, rests and the grind route.

- Switch to the straight line to see the pulls it would cost
- Mobs it never pulls are camps of their own on the map
- With broken gear, every mob that would attack is walked round on the way to the smith

### Under the hood

The rules that keep a character alive and moving, in plain words.

- **Roads first** - Open ground costs three times a road on every path, so STM keeps to the roads where it can.
- **Never too high** - It pulls mobs at most one level above the character, and elites only below its level. A mob that attacks is always fought back.
- **One at a time** - A pull that would bring a second mob is refused, or taken from the edge of the camp with a ranged shot.
- **Casters** - A caster is fought where it stands, and pulled only with no other mob near it.
- **The leash** - A target that runs off is not chased into another camp: STM stops, shoots, and walks back if it does not come.
- **Water** - A fight in the water is taken to the shore when the shore is close, and nothing is pulled from deep water.
- **Broken gear** - With something broken, nothing is crafted, mined or scanned until the repair is paid, and the walk to the smith goes round every mob that would attack.
- **Clear ground** - Rests and crafting happen away from mob spawns, never inside a camp.
- **After a death** - The ghost comes back where no mob stands, after a look round.
- **Stalls** - A wait that goes on too long rings your phone.
- **Money and bags** - Junk is sold before the bags fill, repairs are paid, and whole stacks it does not need are sold.
- **Professions** - Mining, smelting, blacksmithing, first aid and cooking happen along the way.

## For players the grind leaves out

_Who it's for_ - World of Warcraft asks for long sessions of fast, precise key presses. STM is both a leveling tool and an accessibility tool: it takes your character up the levels, and it opens the game to everyone who finds that part hard, painful or simply too long.

- **Limited mobility and one-handed play** - Play WoW with one hand, or with very little movement. STM handles the constant keys and clicks of combat and travel, and you step in whenever you like.
- **Pain, RSI and fatigue** - When WoW with chronic pain or RSI means short sessions, STM carries the repetitive part, so your hands can rest while your character keeps progressing.
- **Busy lives, little time** - Work, family, caring for someone: STM levels your character while you get on with the day, and keeps you posted on your phone.
- **Anyone tired of the grind** - Love the world, not the hundredth wolf? Level up in WoW without grinding for hours, and save your energy for the parts you enjoy.

## Questions, answered honestly

**What is STM?**

STM is one app that is both a leveling tool and an accessibility tool for World of Warcraft: a WoW accessibility addon and desktop companion that takes your character up the levels. While it runs, it plays the repetitive parts of the game for you: walking to enemies, the combat rotation, retreating from a fight it is losing, resting and eating, looting, selling junk, repairing gear, training new spells, gathering and crafting. Questing is coming soon.

**Does STM play the game for me?**

Yes, while it runs. STM controls your character through the repetitive parts of play. You can take over at any moment, and one key pauses it.

**Which games does it work with?**

STM is one app that controls every version: WoW Classic, WoW Anniversary, WoW: Forever and WoW Retail. Hardcore and questing are coming soon. STM runs on a Windows PC next to the game.

**What does beta mean?**

STM is in beta: it already runs and plays, and we are still improving it. It is built to play from the first level up to max level. Some stretches are better tested than others, and the Results section shows exactly which levels have been measured. If you meet a rough edge, tell us on Telegram.

**How much does it cost, and how do I pay?**

STM is paid, with a lower pre-sale price until WoW: Forever launches on 4 November 2026. Message @HugeKush on Telegram for the price and the payment details; you pay there and receive STM with setup help. There is no free version.

**How do I give feedback?**

On Telegram only: send a message to @HugeKush. The feedback section on this page has a ready-made template you can copy.

**Do you collect any data?**

No. This site has no tracking, no cookies, no forms and no sign-up. It only links to Telegram, and nothing is sent unless you send a message yourself.

**Is it allowed by Blizzard, and can my account be affected?**

STM is third-party software and isn't approved by Blizzard. Automating gameplay may go against the game's terms of use and could put your account at risk. You decide whether to use it.

**Do I need to watch it?**

You can, but you do not have to sit at the PC. The in-game panel shows what STM is doing now and next, a dashboard on your PC shows the details, and Telegram sends status, screenshots, clips and a live view to your phone.

**How do I stop it instantly?**

Press Pause in the game, or send /stop to STM on Telegram. Either one stops STM and releases every key.
